Keyboard controls

Choose a control to assign a new key. Right and Left Shift are separate keys. Assignments are saved in this browser where storage is available. Enter (or Numpad Enter) can be assigned to Fire. On the title screen Enter still starts the game, and during initials entry it confirms a letter. Escape cancels key selection.
